Golden Hour Ramen is a small Python program that paints one bowl of steaming ramen, pixel by pixel, for make.fun's "The picture that tastes of one ingredient" bounty ($20 in USDC). No image libraries, no network, just the Python 3.13 standard library.

## The picture

A top-down bowl of ramen on dark warm wood: golden-orange broth with glistening oil, wavy noodles, a glossy ajitama egg, two slices of chashu, nori, spring onion, sesame and a few drops of chili oil, with thin steam rising and wooden chopsticks resting at the rim. The ramen was drawn to look delicious at full size and still read clearly at 300 pixels across, with the warm broth glow as the one flavor the picture is meant to taste of.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

The program writes the picture to `/output/entry.png` (896x896, under 1 MiB).
